# Responsible use

NyxForge's public edition is designed for Safe image and video generation.

Do not use NyxForge to create sexual content, sexualized depictions, intimate
imagery of a real person, non-consensual imagery, exploitative content, or any
content involving minors. Do not bypass the Safe-only controls, API validation,
prompt safeguards, or filtered knowledge base.

You are responsible for the prompts, source media, checkpoints, extensions,
workflows, and outputs you use. Obtain permission before transforming another
person's image, respect privacy and intellectual-property rights, and follow
the policies and laws that apply where you live.

The negative prompt contains safety-denial vocabulary by design. Those terms
reduce unsafe checkpoint drift; they are not generation features.

Security or safety bypasses should be reported privately through the process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not demonstrated with personal or harmful media.
